Most brands are built to be looked at. A logo, a feed, an ad. You glance, and you keep walking.

The brands people stay loyal to work differently. They give you somewhere to be.

I learned this from an album, of all things. Everything about it, from the cover to the stage show, pointed to one place. That's what turned listeners into people who belonged to it.

Three things worth checking in your own brand:
1. Every touchpoint should point to the same idea.
2. At least one touchpoint should be somewhere people can take part, not just watch.
3. Someone should be able to recognise you with the logo covered.

Founders: which touchpoint in your brand still feels like a poster, not a door?
