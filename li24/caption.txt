The best piece of advice I got this year came from one founder who answered a cold message.

It changed how I pitch, and how I think about being useful at all. Not "here's what I do", but "here's what I heard you need, and here's how I'd solve it."

What it looks like in practice:
1. Research the gap between their vision and their reality.
2. Speak to that gap in their words, not yours.
3. Show you can adapt to them, not the other way round.
