Twelve Australian designers took their work to Paris. I went through what they had in common.

Two things showed up every time: serious craft, and a clear story. A third decided who got remembered: how it looked.

I think of it as a lighthouse. The stone is craft, the tower is story, the light is aesthetics. People see the light first, but it only stays standing because of the stone.

For any label with global ambitions:
1. Fix craft and story before you spend on look.
2. Then make the look cohesive everywhere.
3. Remember people buy the world, then the story, then the product.
