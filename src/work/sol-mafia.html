The name

Sol Mafia

Always in blackletter, never in capitals. It sits small in the nav and large only on a hero.

The lips

The drip

Every change of room drips: bone into ink, ink into bone. It’s the house signature, used only at the edges.

Three voices

Sol Mafia

Blackletter. The name only.

Restrained opulence

Condensed serif, in capitals. Every headline.

Pieces for the good life. Coastal nights, late tables and the long drive home.

Clean sans. Everything you read.

True colour

Photos keep their own colour, with no tint and no filter. Our own shoots will replace these board images.

The lifestyle

See the world.

Enjoy the finer things.

Hold life with passion and reverence.

Chase awe, thrills and new heights.

Be unapologetic and fearless.

Ambition drives it: a commitment to excel, and to taste everything life has to offer.
