What a few weeks of figuring it out has taught me. ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏

Lately, everything has been a testament to the faith I put in myself, and my willingness to figure it out.

A year ago I had the ideas. Half-built worlds, brand names, decks I'd never send. What I didn't have was the trust that I could take something from my head and make it real without someone handing me permission first.

That's changed. I don't think it was talent. I think it was a handful of small, boring decisions, repeated until they added up.

The gap

Most of us live in the gap between idea and execution. We can see the thing. We can describe it at dinner. A month later, it's still a description.

I wrote one line down a few weeks ago, and it's stayed pinned above everything since:

“Compression of information and speed of execution towards bottlenecks is a recipe for success.”

In plain words: learn only what the next step needs. Then move fast as fuck at whatever is actually holding you up. Not the fun part. The stuck part.

The hunger

Every time I close that gap, it gets harder to go back to waiting. It's put this hunger in me.

Hunger without structure burns out, though. So the other half of the work is looking after the person doing it.

I think about health in five parts: emotional, physical, mental, relational and spiritual. Each one is a lever, and each one gets a daily discipline. Nothing heroic. Simple and effective is the standard. On a bad day, I know exactly which lever to pull.

Choosing the version

Underneath it all is an idea from a book called Reality Transurfing. You don't force the outcome. You choose the version of your life you want, then move towards it calmly and consistently, without clinging.

I'm not gripping. I'm choosing, then doing the next thing.

If your idea is still a description

1.Name the one bottleneck. Just one.
2.Learn only what you need to move it.
3.Move it this week, before the idea goes cold.

Then do it again.

Faith in yourself isn't a feeling you wait for. It's a receipt. You collect one every time you figure something out.
