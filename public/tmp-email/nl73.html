When did a brand last give you a jolt? ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏ ͏

When did a brand last give you a jolt? Not a nice ad. Not a clever post. The kind of thing that stops you mid-step and stays with you for days.

For me, it's been a while. And I don't think it's because no one's capable.

The mood underneath

We're living through a major shift, and you can feel it. There's disillusionment in the air, and fear underneath it. When no one's sure what's coming, saying nothing much feels like the safe move.

I understand it. But I think the culture needs the opposite. It needs a jolt.

What we're up against

If I had to give the problem a face, it would be a zombie.

Not a monster. Something quieter. Complacency. Conformity. A lack of vitality. The zombie isn't dangerous because it attacks. It's dangerous because it spreads, and because it's thoughtless. You can be very busy and still be one.

That's the real opponent. Not other brands. Not the algorithm. The slow drift into going through the motions.

What a brand can be

I believe brands can be fuel for people to feel more optimistic about the future.

I don't mean slogans about positivity. I mean spectacle. Work that feels larger than life, made with enough conviction that you stop on the street and feel something move.

The stories we grew up on had a shape: a valiant warrior fighting evil. I think we need a new narrative in that spirit. Not loud for the sake of loud. Intense the way sincere things are intense.

Gold in the culture

The good news is, it already exists in small places. There are little pieces of gold hidden in the culture. Those are the sparks. My job, as I see it, is to find them, learn from them, and help more of them catch.

That's what I'm building towards. Something that makes people feel more alive for having been near it.

A small test

Next time you're about to put something out, ask one question: would anyone feel anything if this disappeared tomorrow?

If the answer is no, don't polish it. Make it braver.
