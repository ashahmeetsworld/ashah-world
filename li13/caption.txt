If the only reason to visit your store is to buy something, you're competing with a phone in bed.

The physical stores doing well right now give people a reason that isn't shopping. The good news is that it doesn't take a flagship budget.

A few I've seen work for small labels:
1. A Thursday listening night, where a DJ plays a new record start to finish and nobody's asked to buy a thing.
2. A repair bench on Saturdays: bring in a worn piece and leave with it mended and stitched with your initials.
3. A front table that's always set for coffee, so the regulars have somewhere to land between errands.

The fourth, a corner built to be filmed, is in the carousel.

Which of these could your store add first?
