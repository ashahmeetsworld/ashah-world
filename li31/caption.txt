Artists treat reinvention as part of the job. Most brands treat it as a risk.

I spent a few weeks listening to the catalogues of artists I grew up with, era by era. They reinvent in three distinct ways, and each one is open to brands too.

The three:
1. A new world, the same person.
2. A new persona.
3. A deeper dive into the world you already have.

The rule that holds them together: keep what people recognise, change what makes them look again.

Which kind of reinvention is your brand due for?
