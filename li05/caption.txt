The best hotels don't wait for you to ask. They've thought about the thing before you know you want it.

That's the standard I hold myself to with anyone I work with: constant care, a bit of awe in the atmosphere, and a space where people can let their guard down.

It applies to brands as much as to service. Your community can feel the difference between being sold to and being looked after.

Three ways to bring the standard home:
1. Anticipate one need before your customer has to name it.
2. Design one moment that's quietly beautiful, for no reason except care.
3. Make it easy for people to be honest with you.
