The World Deck

The founding document for Ashah Meets World — the tension, the thesis, the persona, the skill stack and the offer, each told through one illustrated metaphor.

Client
Ashah Meets World
Year
2026
Role
Strategy, worldbuilding, art direction, illustration

The idea

One star is just a light. Connect enough of them and people start to see a shape. The deck treats Ashah Meets World as a living case study: a small set of symbols repeated until the world is recognisable at a glance.

The approach

Every slide gets one metaphor and one illustration: a tempest for the noise, a lagoon for the pull of belief, a door instead of a window, a lighthouse instead of a lantern. Night gradients, italic serif headlines and one hue per skill carry it from start to finish.

What it does

It’s the pitch, the strategy and the brand guidelines in one: the same playbook offered to clients, proven on my own world first.
